**The four concepts become decodable at successively deeper layers: part of speech at layer 4, sentiment at layer 9, named entity type at layer 14 and statement truthfulness at layer 20.** Each line is the test accuracy of a probe trained on activations from one layer of the 24-layer model, measured on 2,000 held-out examples per concept and layer; shaded bands are the confidence intervals reported with each estimate. Dots and drop lines mark the first layer where accuracy reaches 0.75, halfway between chance (dotted line, 0.5) and perfect; all four reach 0.91–0.96 at the final layer.
