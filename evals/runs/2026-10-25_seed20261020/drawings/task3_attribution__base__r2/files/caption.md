**Integrated gradients agrees best with ablation on all three models.** Each point is the Spearman rank correlation, across attention heads, between one attribution method's head scores and the measured effect of ablating each head (GPT-2 small, n = 144 heads; Pythia-410M, n = 384; Gemma-2-2B, n = 208). Horizontal bars show the confidence interval for each correlation. Methods are ordered by their mean correlation across the three models (right margin). The ranking is the same on every model: integrated gradients (ρ = 0.80–0.89) > attribution patching (0.60–0.64) > gradient × input (0.42–0.48) > saliency (0.26–0.33) > activation magnitude (0.15–0.19). The confidence intervals of integrated gradients do not overlap those of any other method. Hollow markers mean weak evidence for a nonzero correlation (Bayes factor BF₁₀ < 10). Only activation magnitude falls in this range (BF₁₀ = 0.8–3.6), so it barely tracks ablation at all. Every other method–model pair has BF₁₀ ≥ 13.8.
