**Figure 1. Concepts become linearly decodable at different depths.** Accuracy of a linear probe trained on each layer's activations, for four binary concepts (chance = 50%, dashed line). Shaded bands are confidence intervals. Markers show each concept's *onset layer*, defined as the first layer at which accuracy reaches halfway between chance and that concept's peak and stays above that level in every later layer. Part of speech becomes decodable first (layer 4), followed by sentiment (layer 9), named entity type (layer 14), and statement truthfulness (layer 20). The first three concepts level off at 93–97% accuracy. Truthfulness is still rising at the final layer (91%), so its peak, and therefore its onset threshold, may be underestimated. Each point is evaluated on 2,000 held-out examples.
