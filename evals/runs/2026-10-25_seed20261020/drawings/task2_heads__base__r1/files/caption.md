**Three attention heads in layers 9–10 account for most of the model's sensitivity to single-head ablation.** Each of the 144 heads (12 layers × 12 heads) was ablated on its own, and the resulting increase in loss was averaged over 500 prompts. **(a)** Mean loss increase for each head, by layer (rows, layer 0 at top) and head index (columns). The color scale is linear and starts at 0. The 20 heads with slightly negative estimates (as low as −0.022) are drawn at 0. **(b)** All heads ranked by mean loss increase. Error bars are 95% confidence intervals (±1.96 × SEM across prompts). Ablating L9.H6 (1.25), L9.H9 (0.91) or L10.H0 (0.72) raises the loss more than ten times as much as ablating any other head. Together these three make up 54% of the summed loss increase over all heads. The other 141 heads range from −0.022 to 0.064 (median 0.017). For 47 of them the confidence interval excludes zero (uncorrected for multiple comparisons), so these heads have small but nonzero effects. No head's ablation significantly lowers the loss.
