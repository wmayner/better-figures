**Integrated gradients agrees best with ablation on all three models.** Each point is the Spearman rank correlation (ρ) between one attribution method's per-head scores and the measured effect of ablating each head, for GPT-2 small (144 heads), Pythia-410M (384 heads), and Gemma-2-2B (208 heads). Horizontal bars show confidence intervals. Methods are sorted by their unweighted mean ρ across the three models, shown at right. The methods rank in the same order on every model. Integrated gradients comes first (ρ = 0.80–0.89), and its intervals do not overlap those of the runner-up, attribution patching (ρ = 0.60–0.64). Activation magnitude agrees least (ρ = 0.15–0.19). Although its intervals stay above zero, the Bayes factors give it little evidence of any correlation (BF₁₀ = 0.8–3.6), whereas every other method has BF₁₀ > 10 on every model.
