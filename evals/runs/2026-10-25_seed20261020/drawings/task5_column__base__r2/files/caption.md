**Figure 1: Concepts become linearly decodable in sequence, from surface syntax to truthfulness.** Linear probe accuracy at each layer for four concepts (n = 2,000 test examples per point; shaded bands are confidence intervals). The dotted line marks chance (0.5). We call a concept decodable at the first layer where accuracy reaches 0.75, halfway between chance and perfect, interpolating linearly between adjacent layers. Markers show these onsets and the numbers beside them give the onset layer: part of speech at 3.4, sentiment at 8.3, named entity type at 13.8, and statement truthfulness at 19.6. After a steep rise, the first three concepts level off at about 0.93–0.95. Statement truthfulness is still rising at the final layer, where it reaches 0.91.
