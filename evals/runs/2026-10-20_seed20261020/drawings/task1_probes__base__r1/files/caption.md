**Figure: Concepts become linearly decodable at different depths, from part of speech to statement truthfulness.** Held-out probe accuracy at each of the model's 24 layers (0–23) for four concepts, with each probe evaluated on 2,000 test examples. Lines show accuracy and shaded bands show confidence intervals. The dashed line marks chance (0.5). We call a concept *decodable* from the first layer at which accuracy reaches 0.75 (dotted line, halfway between chance and perfect) and stays at or above it in every later layer. Markers and labels show that layer for each concept. All four probes start at chance. Part of speech becomes decodable first (layer 4), followed by sentiment (layer 9), named entity type (layer 14) and statement truthfulness (layer 20). The first three level off at about 0.93–0.96. Truthfulness reaches 0.91 at the final layer and is still rising there.
