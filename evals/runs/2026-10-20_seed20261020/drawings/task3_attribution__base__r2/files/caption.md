**Integrated gradients is the attribution method that best matches ablation, in every model tested.** For each of five attribution methods (rows) and three models (colors and markers), we compute the Spearman rank correlation between the method's score for each attention head and that head's measured ablation effect. Points are the correlations and horizontal bars are 95% confidence intervals. Rows are sorted by the average correlation across the three models. The number of heads per model is given in the legend.

Integrated gradients reaches ρ = 0.80 (GPT-2 small), 0.82 (Pythia-410M) and 0.89 (Gemma-2-2B), and its confidence interval does not overlap attribution patching's (ρ = 0.60–0.64) in any model. The five methods rank in the same order in all three models: integrated gradients > attribution patching > gradient × input > saliency > activation magnitude. Activation magnitude agrees only weakly with ablation (ρ = 0.15–0.19). Its confidence intervals exclude zero only narrowly, and the Bayes factors for a nonzero correlation are inconclusive (BF₁₀ = 0.8–3.6). Every other method–model pair has BF₁₀ > 10, and all of them except saliency have BF₁₀ > 10⁶.
