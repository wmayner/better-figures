**Figure 1. The four concepts become decodable at different depths: part of speech by layer 4, sentiment by layer 9, named entity type by layer 15 and statement truthfulness only by layer 21.** All four probes start near 50% accuracy at layer 0 (49–53%). Each concept reaches its plateau of 91–97% within about five layers of leaving 50%. At the last layer (23) the four probes are within 5 points of each other (91% for truthfulness to 96% for sentiment).

Each line is the test accuracy of a probe trained on one layer's activations, at each of the model's 24 layers (0–23), with 2,000 test examples per concept and layer. Shaded bands are the 95% confidence intervals given in the data. We call a concept decodable at the first layer where its probe accuracy reaches 80%. The dotted line marks that threshold, and the dots mark each concept's first layer at or above it.
