**Figure 1. Integrated gradients matched ablation best on all three models.** Its rank correlation with ablation was 0.80 [0.74, 0.85] on GPT-2 small, 0.82 [0.79, 0.85] on Pythia-410M and 0.89 [0.86, 0.92] on Gemma-2-2B. The next best method, attribution patching, reached 0.60 to 0.64. On GPT-2 small the two methods' intervals nearly touch (0.74 against 0.73). Activation magnitude stayed below 0.19 on every model, and its Bayes factors for a nonzero correlation were between 0.8 and 3.6.
Each dot is the Spearman correlation between a method's scores for a model's attention heads and the effect of ablating each head. Lines are 95% confidence intervals. Within each method the three dots are, top to bottom, GPT-2 small (144 heads), Pythia-410M (384 heads) and Gemma-2-2B (208 heads). Methods are ordered by their mean correlation over the three models.
