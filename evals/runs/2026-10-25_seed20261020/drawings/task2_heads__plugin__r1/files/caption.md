**Figure 1. Only 3 of the 144 attention heads, two in layer 9 and one in layer 10, raised the loss by more than 0.1 when ablated.** Ablating L9 H6 raised the loss by 1.25 ± 0.07, L9 H9 by 0.91 ± 0.06 and L10 H0 by 0.72 ± 0.05. No other head raised it by more than 0.064, and the median of the other 141 heads is 0.017.
Each cell is the mean increase in loss when that one head is ablated, over 500 prompts; ± values are standard errors of the mean. Layer 0 is at the bottom. The colour scale starts at 0, so the 20 heads whose ablation lowered the loss slightly (by at most 0.022, within about 1.5 standard errors) are drawn white.
