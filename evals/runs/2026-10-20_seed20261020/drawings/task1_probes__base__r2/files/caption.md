**Concepts become linearly decodable at progressively deeper layers.** Held-out accuracy of a probe trained on each layer's activations (layers 0–23; 2,000 test examples per probe) for four concepts. Lines show accuracy and shaded bands show 95% confidence intervals. The gray line marks chance (50%). A dot marks each concept's *onset layer*, defined as the first layer from which the lower confidence bound stays above chance at every later layer. Part of speech is decodable from layer 1, sentiment from layer 5, named entity type from layer 10 and statement truthfulness from layer 17. The first three concepts each go on to plateau at about 92–96% accuracy, around layers 7, 11 and 17 respectively. Truthfulness reaches 91% at the final layer and has not clearly plateaued.
