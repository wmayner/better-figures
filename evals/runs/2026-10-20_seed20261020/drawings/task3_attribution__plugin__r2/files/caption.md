**Integrated gradients agrees best with ablation on all three models.** Each point is the Spearman rank correlation between a method's attribution scores for the attention heads and the measured effect of ablating each head (n = 144 heads for GPT-2 small, 384 for Pythia-410M, 208 for Gemma-2-2B). Bars are 95% confidence intervals. The order of the five methods is the same on every model, and integrated gradients (ρ = 0.80–0.89) is ahead of the next-best method, attribution patching (ρ = 0.60–0.64), with intervals that do not overlap. Bayes factors (BF₁₀) for a nonzero correlation exceed 10⁶ for the top three methods on every model. For activation magnitude they are only 0.8–3.6, so the evidence that it tracks ablation at all is weak.
