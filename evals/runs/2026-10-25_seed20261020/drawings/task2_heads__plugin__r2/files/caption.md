**Figure 1. Three attention heads, 9.6, 9.9 and 10.0, account for the large loss increases; no other head raises loss by more than 0.07 nats when ablated.** Ablating head 9.6 raises loss by 1.25 ± 0.07 nats, head 9.9 by 0.91 ± 0.06 and head 10.0 by 0.72 ± 0.05 (mean ± SEM). The next largest effect, from head 10.5, is 0.064 ± 0.019 nats. The other 141 heads are each small, but 45 of them exceed twice their standard error, and together their single-head effects add up to 46% of the summed effect over all heads.

Each cell is one of the 144 heads (12 layers × 12 heads, layer 0 at the bottom), shaded by the mean increase in loss when that head alone is ablated, averaged over 500 prompts. Heads above 0.1 nats are named in their cells as layer.head. Twenty heads have small negative values (as low as −0.022 nats); the colour scale starts at 0, so they are drawn white.
