**Figure 1. The four concepts become linearly decodable at successively deeper layers: part of speech at layer 4, sentiment at 9, named entity type at 14 and statement truthfulness at 20.** These onsets are 5 to 6 layers apart. Statement truthfulness rises last and is still climbing at the final layer, where it reaches 91% [89, 93]; the other three level off between 93% and 96%.
Each line is the test accuracy of a probe trained on one layer's activations, and the shaded band is its 95% confidence interval. Each point is evaluated on 2,000 held-out examples. A dot marks the first layer where accuracy reaches 75%, halfway between chance and perfect, and that layer is labelled on the x-axis. The dotted line is chance (50%).
