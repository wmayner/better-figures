**Figure N. Probes decode the four concepts starting at layers 4, 9, 14 and 20, in that order: part of speech, then sentiment, named entity type and statement truthfulness.** Each concept rises from below 0.55 to above 0.9 accuracy within six or seven layers. At the last layer, accuracy is 0.91 to 0.96 for all four.
Each line is the test accuracy of a probe trained at that layer, measured on 2,000 test examples per concept and layer. Shaded bands are the confidence intervals. A dot and its number mark the layer where a concept becomes decodable, defined as the first layer from which accuracy stays at or above 0.75 to the last layer. The dotted line marks chance accuracy (0.5).
