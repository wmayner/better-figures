**Figure: Concepts become decodable at successively deeper layers.** Probe accuracy at each layer (0–23) for four concepts, evaluated on 2,000 held-out test examples per concept and layer. Lines show accuracy; shaded bands show the confidence interval reported for each point (`ci_low`–`ci_high`). The solid gray line marks chance (50%), and the dotted line marks the onset threshold of 75% accuracy, halfway between chance and perfect accuracy. Dots mark the layer where each concept's accuracy first crosses this threshold, interpolated linearly between adjacent layers: part of speech at layer 3.4, sentiment at 8.3, named entity type at 13.8, and statement truthfulness at 19.6. Every concept is at chance in the earliest layers, rises steeply over about five layers, and then levels off. Part of speech, sentiment and named entity type each plateau at about 93–96%. Statement truthfulness emerges last and is still rising at the final layer (91% at layer 23).
