**Integrated gradients agrees best with ablation on all three models.** For each attribution method and model, the point is the Spearman rank correlation between the method's per-head attribution scores and the measured effect of ablating each head (n = 144 heads for GPT-2 small, 384 for Pythia-410M, 208 for Gemma-2-2B). Within each method the rows are, from top to bottom, GPT-2 small, Pythia-410M and Gemma-2-2B, and error bars are confidence intervals. Integrated gradients reaches ρ = 0.80–0.89, and its intervals sit above those of the next-best method, attribution patching (ρ = 0.60–0.64), on every model. Activation magnitude barely tracks ablation (ρ = 0.15–0.19; Bayes factors BF₁₀ = 0.8–3.6, so little evidence of any correlation).
