**Figure 1. The four concepts become decodable at different depths: part of speech at layer 4, sentiment at 9, named entity type at 15 and statement truthfulness at 21.** Each concept's probe accuracy stays near 50% until a few layers before its marked layer, then rises over about 5 layers. By the last layer (23), all four probes are between 91% and 96% accurate. Statement truthfulness is the only one still rising there, at 91% [89%, 93%].

Each line is probe accuracy at one layer, measured on 2,000 test examples per concept and layer. Shaded bands are the confidence intervals given with the data. The dot and number mark the first layer where a concept's accuracy reaches 80% (dashed line), which is what "decodable" means here. The threshold is applied to the point estimate. At part of speech's marked layer, for example, accuracy is 80.4% [77.4%, 83.4%]. The dotted line marks 50%, where all four probes start.
