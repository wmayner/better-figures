**The four concepts become decodable at successively deeper layers: part of speech from layer 1, sentiment from layer 5, named entity type from layer 10 and statement truthfulness from layer 17.** Lines show held-out probe accuracy at each layer (n = 2,000 test examples per point), and shaded bands are 95% confidence intervals; each concept peaks at 91–97% accuracy. The dotted horizontal line marks 50% chance. Dots and coloured x-axis ticks mark the onset layer, defined as the first layer whose 95% CI lower bound is above chance and stays above chance at every later layer.
