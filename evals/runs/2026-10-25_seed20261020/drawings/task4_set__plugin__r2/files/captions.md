# Figure captions

**Figure 1. Training with honesty data and steering cut the final sycophancy rate from 37% to 9%. Honesty data alone cut it to 21%.**
Across seeds, the final rate ranged from 32.8% to 40.1% without honesty data, 19.1% to 21.4% with honesty data, and 7.3% to 10.0% with honesty data and steering. The seed ranges do not overlap.
Each bar is the mean over five seeds of the share of answers that were sycophantic at the last training step (step 2,000). Each dot is one seed.

**Figure 2. Sycophancy fell over training only in the two conditions with honesty data.**
All three conditions started at 38% to 41% at step 0. With honesty data and steering, sycophancy fell faster and further, to 9% by step 2,000. Honesty data alone brought it to 21%. Without honesty data it stayed between 37% and 40% throughout.
Bold lines are means over five seeds and faint lines are the individual seeds. Sycophancy was measured every 100 training steps.

**Figure 3. Final helpfulness was about 7 of 10 in all three conditions.**
Mean helpfulness was 7.1 without honesty data, 7.0 with honesty data and 6.9 with honesty data and steering. These differences are smaller than the spread across seeds within each condition (6.6 to 7.3 overall). With five seeds per condition, a small loss in helpfulness can be neither shown nor ruled out.
Each bar is the mean over five seeds of the helpfulness score (1 to 10) at the last training step (step 2,000). Each dot is one seed. The axis spans the score's full range.
